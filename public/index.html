Our Philosophy

Every bag begins on a mountainside we've actually visited.

01

Ember & Rind started in a converted garage with a two-kilo drum roaster and a stubborn belief: that coffee this good shouldn't require a chemistry degree to understand. Just heat, time, and attention.

"We don't chase trends. We chase the moment a lot tastes exactly like the hillside it grew on."

Today we still roast in small batches, still cup every single lot blind before it earns a place on the shelf, and still drive out to the farms ourselves — because a relationship you can't see doesn't belong in your cup.

Mara Okonkwo, Head Roaster

Brew Guide

Four steps between a good bag and a great cup.

01

Grind Fresh

Grind just before brewing — medium-coarse for pour-over, like raw sugar. Pre-ground coffee loses aroma within minutes.

02

Weigh It Out

16:1 water-to-coffee ratio is our house standard. A kitchen scale matters more than any gadget you'll buy.

03

Bloom First

Pour double the coffee's weight in water at 94°C, wait 30 seconds. Let the grounds off-gas before the real pour.

04

Pour Slow

Circular pours, center to edge, over three minutes total. Rushing the pour is the single most common mistake.
